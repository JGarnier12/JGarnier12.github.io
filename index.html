ServiceFuze Backup Utility

A private, single-operator tool that keeps encrypted backups of its operator's own software development work.

What it is

This is not a product and is not offered to anyone else. It is a set of scripts run on one personal computer by one person, who is also its only user. It exists to protect that person's own software development repository — in particular work that exists only on that computer and has not yet been published anywhere.

What it does

  • Creates a compressed archive of the operator's own development repository.
  • Encrypts that archive on the operator's computer, before anything leaves it.
  • Uploads the encrypted archive to two Google Drive accounts belonging to the operator, so that the loss of one account or computer does not lose the work.
  • Verifies each uploaded copy by reading it back and recomputing its checksum.

What it asks of Google Drive

Exactly one permission: drive.file. That grants access only to files this utility itself creates. It cannot see, read, modify or delete any other file in the account — including the operator's own unrelated documents.

Who it is for

The operator, and nobody else. There is no sign-up, no service, no customers and no other users. If you have arrived here from a Google consent screen, it is because you are the operator.

Privacy

The archives are encrypted before upload, and the decryption key is held only by the operator. It is never given to Google and is never held by the part of this utility that creates and uploads archives, so their contents are not readable by Google or by anyone with access to the Drive accounts. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
